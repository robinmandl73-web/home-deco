2. Website hosting (Cloudflare)

This website uses Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), including Cloudflare Workers for hosting and DNS, proxy and security services. When you access the website, technical connection data may be processed, such as your IP address, date and time, requested URL, and technical browser and connection information. This is necessary to operate the website reliably and protect it from attacks; the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). Cloudflare may process data outside the EEA. The specific international transfer safeguards and retention periods applicable to our account still need to be checked against the current service terms and settings. More information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

3. Contact and contact form (Formspree)

If you contact us by email, phone or through our contact form, we process your contact details and message to respond to your enquiry. The form requires your name, email address, preferred contact method and message. Your phone number is required only if you request a callback. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual enquiries or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries (legitimate interest in responding).

We use Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) to transmit form submissions. When you submit the form, your information is transferred to Formspree and forwarded to us. This may entail transfers to a country outside the EEA. The transfer safeguards, data processing agreement and exact retention periods applying to our Formspree account still need verification against the service agreement and settings. See Formspree's Privacy Policy. We retain enquiries only as long as necessary to process them, document relevant business correspondence and meet any applicable legal retention obligations.

4. Google Fonts

The website currently loads “DM Sans” and “Playfair Display” from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited and/or Google LLC). Your browser establishes a connection with Google servers, which involves transferring technical connection data such as your IP address. The purpose is consistent website typography; the legal basis relied upon is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Whether this specific remote integration is permissible without prior consent requires separate assessment. Hosting fonts locally is planned to reduce data sharing. See Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google Maps

The map does not load automatically. It is embedded only when you click “Load Google Maps”. This can transmit your IP address and other technical data to Google, potentially including transfers outside the EEA. The legal basis for loading the map following your active choice is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You can use the rest of the website without opening the map. See Google Privacy Policy.

6. Instagram and external links

This website contains links to Instagram but does not automatically embed Instagram content. Clicking a link takes you to the external platform, where its operator is responsible for processing your information.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

As currently implemented, this website does not use its own analytics or advertising tracking. The hosting provider may use strictly necessary security cookies or similar technologies. Any specific technologies in use should be verified against the Cloudflare account configuration.

8. Recipients, retention and provision of data

Recipients of personal data may include the service providers needed for website operation and enquiry handling, particularly Cloudflare and Formspree. Retention depends on the processing purpose and any applicable statutory retention requirements. Information submitted through the contact form is needed to handle your enquiry; without the required contact details, we may be unable to respond. You are not legally required to use the contact form.

9. Your rights

Where applicable, you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time with future effect. Contact us using the details above to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority. According to the website's current implementation, no solely automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.
